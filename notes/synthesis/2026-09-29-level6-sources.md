# Level 6 synthesis, 2026-09-29: the (ii) sources as read

Companion to notes/synthesis/2026-09-29-level6-synthesis.md, as design v2 FINAL requires (C2, amendment 1). One entry
per source marked (ii). For each: the full citation; the DOI; the URLs actually opened on 2026-09-29, namely the
Crossref record (bibliographic check) and the PubMed abstract (text read); a short verbatim quote of the abstract text
the claim rests on; and a paraphrase of the rest of what the claim uses. Quotes are kept short (under 15 words each)
and the remainder is paraphrased. Only abstracts were read; the publisher pages returned an error page or HTTP 403 when
tried during the design (elifesciences.org, nature.com, science.org), so nothing here comes from figures or full text.

## S1. Alvarez-Salvado et al 2018

- Citation: Alvarez-Salvado E, Licata AM, Connor EG, McHugh MK, King BM, Stavropoulos N, Victor JD, Crimaldi JP, Nagel
  KI. Elementary sensory-motor transformations underlying olfactory navigation in walking fruit-flies. eLife 2018;
  7:e37815.
- DOI: 10.7554/eLife.37815
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.7554/eLife.37815;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=30129438&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "an upwind run during odor (ON response), and a local search at odor offset"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: wind orientation requires antennal mechanoreceptors; the search is driven by odour alone.
- Used in: B1, B2, B4; circuit rows M4, M5.

## S2. Demir et al 2020

- Citation: Demir M, Kadakia N, Anderson HD, Clark DA, Emonet T. Walking Drosophila navigate complex plumes using
  stochastic decisions biased by the timing of odor encounters. eLife 2020; 9:e57524.
- DOI: 10.7554/eLife.57524
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.7554/eLife.57524;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=33140723&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "whose direction was biased upwind by the timing of prior odor encounters"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: turns are stochastic saccades of constant magnitude and rate; the eLife plain-language
  summary (in the same PubMed record) says the flies use a strategy different from surge and cast, which they use for
  regular odour streams. The second point comes from the digest, not the abstract.
- Used in: B3.

## S3. Seelig and Jayaraman 2015

- Citation: Seelig JD, Jayaraman V. Neural dynamics for landmark orientation and angular path integration. Nature
  2015; 521(7551):186-191.
- DOI: 10.1038/nature14446
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1038/nature14446;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=25971509&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "relying on self-motion cues in darkness"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: with visual and self-motion cues both absent, the orientation representation is
  maintained through persistent activity; population dynamics are suggestive of ring attractors.
- Used in: B5; circuit row M2.

## S4. Kim et al 2017

- Citation: Kim SS, Rouault H, Druckmann S, Jayaraman V. Ring attractor dynamics in the Drosophila central brain.
  Science 2017; 356(6340):849-853.
- DOI: 10.1126/science.aal4835
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1126/science.aal4835;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=28473639&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "maintained by the circuit with naturalistic dynamics"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: an artificial bump written optogenetically is maintained; local excitation and global
  inhibition enforce a unique, persistent heading representation. The abstract does not state switching between
  competing stimuli or jumps; that claim is (i), from the Phase 6 reading (phase6_synthesis.md:29-31).
- Used in: B12; circuit row M2.

## S5. Felsenberg et al 2018

- Citation: Felsenberg J, Jacob PF, Walker T, Barnstedt O, Edmondson-Stait AJ, Pleijzier MW, Otto N, Schlegel P,
  Sharifi N, Perisse E, Smith CS, Lauritzen JS, Costa M, Jefferis GSXE, Bock DD, Waddell S. Integration of parallel
  opposing memories underlies memory extinction. Cell 2018; 175(3):709-722.e15.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2018.08.021
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.cell.2018.08.021;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=30245010&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "omission of punishment is remembered as a positive experience"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: extinction of aversive memory requires specific dopaminergic neurons; traces of the
  original aversive memory and of a new appetitive extinction memory co-exist in the MBON network and are combined
  within MBONs that direct avoidance. Reacquisition is not reported in the abstract.
- Used in: B9; circuit row M1.

## S6. van Breugel and Dickinson 2014

- Citation: van Breugel F, Dickinson MH. Plume-tracking behavior of flying Drosophila emerges from a set of distinct
  sensory-motor reflexes. Current Biology 2014; 24(3):274-286.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2013.12.023
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.cub.2013.12.023;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=24440395&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "flies initiate a series of vertical and horizontal casts"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: about 190 ms after encountering a plume flies speed up and turn upwind, using visual cues
  for wind direction; the casts begin about 450 ms after losing the plume and keep a crosswind heading.
- Used in: B1, B2; circuit row M4.

## S8. Okubo et al 2020

- Citation: Okubo TS, Patella P, D'Alessandro I, Wilson RI. A neural network for wind-guided compass navigation.
  Neuron 2020; 107(5):924-940.e18.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.06.022
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.06.022;

## S9. Turner-Evans et al 2017

- Citation: Turner-Evans D, Wegener S, Rouault H, Franconville R, Wolff T, Seelig JD, Druckmann S, Jayaraman V.
  Angular velocity integration in a fly heading circuit. eLife 2017; 6:e23496.
- DOI: 10.7554/eLife.23496
- Opened: https://api.crossref.org/works/10.7554/eLife.23496;
  https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=28530551&rettype=abstract&retmode=text
- Quote: "updating the fly's heading representation when the animal turns in darkness"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: a population conjunctively encoding heading and angular velocity, excited selectively by

- Used in: B6; circuit row M3.

## S12. Yang et al 2023 (found at writing; used for the added row B15, not for B10)

- Citation: Yang Q, Zhou J, Wang L, Hu W, Zhong Y, Li Q. Spontaneous recovery of reward memory through active
  forgetting of extinction memory. Current Biology 2023; 33(5):838-848.e3.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2023.01.022
- Opened: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=36731465&rettype=abstract&retmode=text;
  https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.cub.2023.01.022
- Quote: "the extinguished reward memory in Drosophila recovers spontaneously"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: extinction training forms an aversive memory that is actively forgotten (Rac1/Dia);
  blocking that forgetting prevents spontaneous recovery. Reacquisition after extinction (savings) is not reported, so
  B10 stays PENDING.
- Used in: B15 (added at writing).

## S13. DasGupta et al 2014 (found at writing; used for B13)

- Citation: DasGupta S, Ferreira CH, Miesenbock G. FoxP influences the speed and accuracy of a perceptual decision in
  Drosophila. Science 2014; 344(6186):901-904.
- DOI: 10.1126/science.1252114
- Opened: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=24855268&rettype=abstract&retmode=text;
  https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1126/science.1252114
- Quote: "reaction times increased and perceptual accuracy declined"
- Paraphrase of the rest used: in trained odour discriminations, lower stimulus contrast gives longer reaction times
  and lower accuracy, in agreement with a drift-diffusion model (evidence accumulated to a threshold before
  commitment). Declining to choose (abstention) is not reported.
- Used in: B13.
