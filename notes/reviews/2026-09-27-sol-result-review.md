## What the diagnosis shows

**The no-decay layouts differ materially under the same inputs.** Replaying each of the H12, gate-only and H11 full contact/reward histories into A0, A1 and P gives an A0-versus-A1 value difference above `1e-12` first at step 606, with maximum absolute difference `0.2036079284`. The A0/A1 pair has 7,194–7,198 sign-inverted row-steps depending on donor tape; these are repeated time samples, not independent animals. A1 versus P first differs at step 601, reaches maximum absolute difference `0.3174475875`, and has no sign-inverted row-steps in these tapes. In the closed loop, H11 full and gate-only depart on 350/400 rows and reach the same `0.2036079284` maximum value gap. Thus the earlier “last-bit identity” interpretation is false. The fixed-tape contrasts separate module updates under each observed history; their differences across donor tapes show why the H12-versus-gate-only closed-loop contrast cannot be assigned uniquely to decay.

**P3 was masked navigation with continued learning, not silent retention.** In P3 the H12 arm had 4,631 V-source-contact steps in 267/400 rows; gate-only had 4,342 in 248/400. The learning code is supplied by V source contact after movement, even when V navigation whiffs are masked. Those histories differ between arms. This is an input-contract finding, not a claim that source-contact teaching was an implementation error.

**Pure retention changes value from matched A1 checkpoints, with important exceptions.** At the fixed pre-step-2400 boundary, all 400 A1 rows were cloned with their weights, code and traces. The D=0 clones agree; decay-off values and acquisition weights stay fixed under zero code and zero external reinforcement. With decay on, the median V value is `0.374417` at D=0, `0.507910` at D=1200 and `0.767818` at D=5000. Counts above the `+0.5` competitor are 52, 208 and 384; 332 rows cross upward from below by D=5000. The signed recovery ratio's denominator is valid in 381/400 rows; its median on those 381 is `0.213391` at D=1200 and `0.632157` at D=5000. The other 19 had P1 and P2 value `+1`, acquisition component `+1`, and parallel component zero, so there was no measured extinction gap to divide by. They remain in every all-row count.

Nine rows have negative V value at D=0, D=1200 and D=5000, despite P1 value `+1` in each. The [read-only retention inspection](../../experiments/h12/diagnosis/retention-inspection.json) finds a negative **acquisition** component at the P2 checkpoint in all nine. Seven become more negative as a positive parallel component decays; rows 15 and 164 become less negative but remain below zero. The analytical prediction `acq_P2 + par_P2 × (1 - 1/5000)^D` matches the stored retained values to maximum error `3.43e-14` at D=5000. This explains the sign pattern without a new simulation. Acquisition weights are invariant only during the new zero-input retention interval; the embodied P2 history had already changed the acquisition component in these rows. “Parallel memory restores the original positive sign” is therefore not true for all assigned rows under this protocol. The 9 negative and 19 denominator-invalid rows must remain visible in any next experiment.

**P4 had little V sensory opportunity, even in the ceiling control.** V was never whiffed in 332/400 H12 rows, 327/400 gate-only rows and 335/400 ceiling rows. H12 had 68 rows with a V whiff, 57 with a V-supported upwind navigation event, 55 with V contact and 33 with V dwell majority; gate-only had 73, 15, 20 and 10 respectively. The ordered, exhaustive opportunity partition is in the summary. It also reports filter eligibility, both-channel navigation ambiguity, flee overrides, and zero-contact versus positive-contact ties. Contact can occur without a preceding V-supported navigation event, so the partition's ordered labels should not be read as mutually exclusive causal stages. The ceiling result makes the old Stage 2 task's accessibility limit concrete, but these arms still have different histories and positions. It does not by itself establish which changed value would alter choice under matched opportunity.

## Disposition and next work

Package D's specified diagnosis is complete: original provenance, instrumentation identity, three-donor fixed-input replays, all-row A1 retention clones, and P4 opportunity accounting passed remotely. It is measurement only. The registered H12 Stage 1 PASS and Stage 2 STOPPED/UNREADABLE remain unchanged; no new behavioral PASS or adoption follows.

The next experiment should be separately registered as Package E before any new behavioral seed is spent: take every fixed A1 P2 checkpoint, retain matched decay-on and decay-off clones with D=5000 primary and D=0/1200 controls, then place each value into the same reset body and balanced two-plume world with matched random streams and frozen module state. Count V dwell majority over all assigned rows, report nine sign-inverted and 19 denominator-invalid checkpoints without exclusion, and require D=0 field identity. That would test expression of stored value when both sources are accessible. Autonomous return after plume loss and recognition-to-credit assignment require separate tests.
