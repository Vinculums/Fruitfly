## 1. Hypothesis

Stage A tests no hypothesis about the ring and has no pass or fail on it. It registers readings that separate candidate causes of one measured fact: **with the wind cue absent 50 steps at a time in an open plane, the adopted ring (RingExact, tau 1, sigma 0.5, vgain 1, n 16, wind cue 6.0) driving the `return` rule leaves 64.5 percent of agents unrecovered in the last third, where a perfect integrator fed the same rotations leaves 0.5 percent** (K5 record:174, :180), although the ring is more than 45 degrees wrong on only 0.35 percent of cue-off steps and 10.0 degrees wrong on average (K5 record:179). Its output is which Stage B branch the record supports (section 3), as the T3b diagnosis's section 6 did.

### 2.5 The 10 degree mean, read as arithmetic only (inferred; not taken as the cause)

The instruction forbids taking the 10-degree mean as the cause. The arithmetic below is why the tail and the persistence must be measured rather than presumed: the two recorded aggregates cannot tell a tail mechanism from plain diffusion. **Assume** the cue-off error is a Gaussian random walk starting at 0 at each cue-off onset with per-step sd s. Then E abs(e_k) = s sqrt(k) sqrt(2/pi), and its mean over k = 1..50 is about 0.798 x 4.78 s = 3.81 s; the measured 10.0 degrees gives **s about 2.6 degrees per step** and an end-of-block sd of about 18.5 degrees. The share over 45 degrees, 2(1 - Phi(45 / (2.62 sqrt(k)))), is about 1.5 percent at k 50, 0.7 at 40, 0.2 at 30 and under 0.01 below 20; averaged over the block it is **about 0.3 percent, against the measured 0.35** (K5 record:179). Over 90 degrees the same walk gives a z of 4.9 even at k 50: **essentially no agent-steps**. The crosswind displacement such a walk makes over one 50-step block at SPEED 0.6 (ph9.py:31), SPEED x the sum of e_k in radians, has sd 0.6 x 0.01745 x 2.62 x sqrt(50 x 51 x 101 / 6) = 0.6 x 0.01745 x 2.62 x 207.2, **about 5.7 units**, against a plume half-width W0 + SLOPE x along of 2.75 at along 5 and 6.0 at along 18 (ph9.py:34; the start's along range, ph12.py:47). So under that assumption the recorded tail is the walk's own tail, and a persistent small error alone would move an agent about one plume half-width per cue-off block (small-angle, straight-path approximation; the cast's turning changes the projection). **Inferred.** This makes (vi) a candidate to test, not a conclusion; F1, F2 and F-vi separate it from a tail cause and from (i), (ii).

### 2.6 Why the recorded ring and integrator rows are not paired (read)

The ring is built on the agent's generator (ph12.py:84) and draws 200 x 16 normals per ring step (ph10.py:73), twice per agent-step, before the turn noise is drawn (ph12.py:124, :128). `integrate` and `none` draw nothing in estimate. So the world stream is shared (one uniform per agent per step, ph9.py:85; the phases, ph12.py:55) and the source and start are the same, but **the ring arm's turn-noise sequence differs from the integrator's from step 0**. A 'time of divergence' read on the recorded arms is step 0 by construction. **Read, inferred.** Pairing needs the ring's noise on its own stream (`ring_sep`, section 6); its aggregates are a second draw of the same ring on the same worlds, and their agreement with the recorded ring's is reported.

## 7. Criteria (readability) and registered readings

**Readability gates** (all must hold, else section 8): **(R0)** `python src/ph12b.py eval`, unchanged, reproduces the K5 record line for line, all 189 lines; **(R1)** the H18 harness, driven by ph12b's own World3, Nav3 and generators, adds no random draw to any recorded arm, reproduces K5 record:162-189 character for character, and its per-agent arrays (score, whiff, lost_end, err, err_off, abs_off, mismatch) equal run2's (ph12b.py:73-102) bitwise; **(I1)** mismatch count 0 on every agent-step of `ring` and `integrate` (ph12b.py:95-96); **(I2)** `integrate` equals `exact` on every per-agent score; **(I3)** `integrate` with the shadow attached equals `integrate` bitwise; **(I4)** `ring_made` against `ring`: the maximum abs difference of est and of the per-agent scores, reported (w.rot equals the turn up to float rounding, ph12b.py:61, so bitwise equality is not required); **(I5)** the number of agent-steps on which `wind_on.any()` is False (predicted 0), and the number of cue-off agent-steps; **(I6)** the instrument check of ph12b.py:207-209. Pass probability about 1 if the implementation and environment are right; a failure is an implementation or environment error, repaired and disclosed before any reading is taken.

**Definitions.** e_t = angdiff(est_t, head_t), signed, at the step est is computed (as ph12b.py:91). A loss event starts at the agent's last whiff t_L before since reaches 143; it is **unrecovered** if no whiff follows by the last step (about 165 events, 2.1), **recovered** otherwise (about 2376). The pre-loss window is the cue-off steps in [t_L - 50, t_L]; the post-whiff window [t_L + 1, t_L + 142]. Agent level: unrecovered agents are the late-unrecovered ones (ph12.py:164; about 129), recovered agents the other 71. A group under 50 is unreadable. Every reading is printed for `ring`, `ring_sep` and `shadow`, and for `integrate` where defined; the F rows read `ring_sep` (paired) unless stated, with `ring` beside it.

**Reported readings (no threshold):**
- **(A) tail**, per agent-step: shares of cue-off agent-steps with abs(e) over 10, 20, 30, 45, 90, 135 degrees (denominator: cue-off agent-steps), quantiles 50/90/99/99.9/max; per agent: the share of agents with any cue-off step over 45, 90, 135, the per-agent maximum and per-agent share over 20; the same by cue-off step k (1..50).
- **(B) persistence**: run lengths of consecutive cue-off steps with abs(e) over 20 and over 45 (quantiles, maximum); autocorrelation of signed e within cue-off blocks at lags 1, 5, 10, 25; per-block mean signed e and its sd across blocks; **abs(e) on cue-on steps 1..10 after each cue-off block, step by step** (median, p90, share over 20); carry-over: the correlation of e at a block's last cue-off step with e at the next block's first cue-off step, and abs(e) at cue-on step 50.
- **(C) before a loss**: for unrecovered and recovered events, e at t_L, abs(e) median and maximum over the pre-loss and post-whiff windows, the cue state and cast clock at t_L; the per-block path error P = SPEED x sum of sin(e_k) over each cue-off block (a proxy for the crosswind displacement the error makes, 2.5); paired with `integrate` on the same world and turn noise (`ring_sep` only): **the time of divergence** t_div (the first step whose whiff outcome differs within the pair, and the first step at which their positions are more than 1.0 unit apart), e at t_div, and whether t_div precedes t_L.
- **(D) mechanism**: the closed-loop step residual r_t = angdiff(pos_t, pos_(t-1)) - v_t on cue-off steps, its mean and sd by abs(v) bins 0-10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-40, over 40, and the OLS slope of r on v; amp() by cue-off step k; single-step jumps abs(r_t) over 30 degrees per 100,000 cue-off steps; the benches (b0), (b1).
- **(E) exposure**: section 5.

## 10. Predictions, with the arithmetic

- **R0, R1, I1-I6:** pass (read: the harness is the recorded code plus reads). I5: 0 steps without a cued agent (every agent is cued for t < 50; after that all 200 phases would have to fall in one 50-step half, a negligible chance).
- **(A) tail:** over 45 degrees on about 0.35 percent of cue-off steps, about 1,870 agent-steps (measured, 2.2); over 90 and 135 degrees essentially 0 (2.5, inferred). If errors over 90 degrees appear in more than a few agent-steps, the random-walk reading of 2.5 is wrong and a jump mechanism (F-ii) is the first suspect.
- **F1:** NOT MET predicted. Under 2.5's walk a 50-step block ever exceeds 45 degrees with about twice its end-step chance, about 3 percent; a loss window holds about two cue-off blocks, so about 6 percent of events of either kind (inferred).
- **F2:** lag-10 autocorrelation about 0.85 for a random walk at k 25 (sqrt(25/35)), and a snap at cue return (the near-zero cue-on share of 2.2): MET predicted (inferred).
- **F3:** above 0.60 if the loss is cue-driven, which the K5(a) against K5(b) contrast suggests (measured contrast, inferred reading).
- **F-i:** NOT MET predicted (the single-reflection result of 2.4, measured in another arena; the K5(a) pass).
- **F-ii:** not predictable from the record: amp is not recorded, and K5(a) has no cue-free steps. With the state replaced by the recurrent response every step (ph10.py:77), a steady amplitude is the guess (inferred, weak).
- **F-v:** about sqrt(2) = 1.41 if the second step's noise adds independently and is not damped by the normalisation (inferred); MET predicted, but it gates B-v only with F-i and F-ii NOT MET.
- **F-vi:** MET predicted if 2.5 holds: a per-block displacement sd of about 5.7 units against half-widths 2.75 to 6.0 (inferred).
- **What would make them wrong:** errors correlated with the cast's saturated turns (the residual by abs(v) bin in (D)) would move the cause to (i); a bump that loses amplitude through a block would move it to (ii); unrecovered events whose last whiff falls on cue-on steps (F3 NOT MET) would mean the loss is not a cue-loss effect as sketched, and no branch applies as written.
