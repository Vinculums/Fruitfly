## 1. Hypothesis

**H17 Run 2: an agent that, after a registered silence of S = 250 steps with nothing held and no whiff of any odour, replaces the return cast's target with Run 1's crosswind cast of growing amplitude slanted slowly along the wind, finds a plume from an odour-free position outside it where the adopted agent does not, and leaves the adopted behaviour unchanged wherever a whiff arrives within S.** At +1/0 and +1/-1 on Agent10 (H23 filter + H25 release): (i) from the constructed R3 stranded state it senses a plume and reaches a source in a registered fraction of rows against the adopted agent's floor [bench (c1)]; (ii) it does not engage during normal tracking in the H21 task [bench (d); T1]; (iii) in the +1/-1 stranded condition it recovers stranded rows, and that recovery does not pass through the wall reflex [T3]. ### 3.2 The wall contacts (T3 (c))

- **(i) CHOSEN: keep the leg rule and re-anchor the T3 (c) bar.** The measurement says the contacts do not carry the effect: 0 transitions in the 39 contact rows, 0/99 contacts followed by a whiff, all 37 out-of-lost rows in rows without a contact (diag:294, diag:316-322). Run 1's bar (<= 0.10 contacts per row) stood in for 'recovery without the reflex' (Run 1 section 7 M3 (c)); the standing rule of the Phase 7.2 close ('a criterion that stands in for a behavioural claim must be behavioural') points to barring that claim directly. The re-anchored bar (section 7 M3 (c)) guards that the lost-row gain does not pass through a wall contact; it no longer bars the count of contacts, which is reported (predicted 0.15-0.30 per row at S 250: the leg-3 excursion is unchanged and the return cast's extra 40 steps move the agent about +-12 crosswind (exact heading, depending on the cast side), so the wall distance at engagement shifts in both directions).
- **(ii) Rejected: cap the leg amplitude at leg 3** (legs stay 90 steps from k = 3). The envelope becomes [-17.4, +34.8] about the engagement line, +34.8 on leg 1's side only. The contacts are made at the leg-3 amplitude (u 172/185/194, legs 3-4, diag:288), with the wall 26.4-32.5 away (diag:299), so the cap does not remove them. It does remove reach: in (c1) the side is random (cast_sign, ph16.py:35-39), so in about half the rows only -17.4 points toward the plume, against a gap of 16.5-41.7 outward at engagement (bench (c1) engagement position) minus the cone's half-width 4.5-6.5 at d_along 12-20; the R3 gap 21-28 at the release (Run 1 section 2) is not bridged by 17.4. A cap at leg 2 (+-17.4) would stop the contacts and lose the reach in most rows.
- **(iii) Rejected: the first search leg on the side opposite the last flee.** flee_side is own state: drawn once at construction (ph11.py:127), flipped only by the wall reflex (ph11.py:140), read only while a negative hold is kept (ph21.py:80); nothing clears it at a release (ph24.py:62 touches `silence` only). So it is still set after the release and, since Agent10 makes no contact during the flee (R3: 0.000 contacts per row), it still points the way the agent fled. The rule would be sigma_1 = sin(flee_side) (flee_side 90, +y, gives sigma +1, target 255, toward -y; ph26.py:74-76). Arithmetic at S 250 (exact heading): the 39 contact rows sit 39.4-41.9 from the nearer axis (diag:299); with leg 3 toward the plume they would reach +34.8 at u 180, at d_along about 14 where the cone's half-width is 5.0, i.e. 39.8 from the axis at the very end of the leg, a grazing pass; leg 4 then goes 34.8 toward the flee side and meets the same wall at about u 290 instead of u 175, and leg 5's parity flip turns the agent back into it. So in the far rows the gain is small and the contacts move to legs 4-5 rather than vanish; in the rows whose leg 1 already pointed at the plume nothing changes; in T1, W1 and T3a flee_side is a random constant, as cast_sign is now. The gain is inferred, not measured (the diagnosis did not print sigma_1 against flee_side), and it would be a second mechanism change in a Run 2 whose measured reason is (d). It stays an option for a later run, informed by (h2)'s new reported numbers (section 4).
- **(iv) Rejected: a search-owned side fixed at engagement** (so the reflex reverses the current leg without the next parity flip turning the agent back). It needs a second state variable beyond q, hence a new relaxation, for a pattern that carries no transition.
