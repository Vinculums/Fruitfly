## 4. Reading (interpretation, not measured)

- The search runs as specified. Where it finds no whiff is mainly a matter of which way its first leg points. The rows without a whiff sit about 29 outward of the negative source (they had fled away from the valued one). In the stranded rows that engage in time, leg 1, and so leg 3, points to the flee side in 65 of 84, away from the pair. Their first crossing of the pair then comes at the end of leg 4 (u about 292), after the upwind slant has carried them upwind of both sources, where nothing is sensed. In (c1), where leg 1 points toward the pair in 194 of 400 rows, those rows whiff 184 of 194 times. This diagnosis does not establish why the cast side matches the flee side in the early-released rows. The only measured link is that equal-side rows release earlier.
- The prediction chain missed on the whiff fraction after engagement: 0.21 against 0.5-0.7 (Run 1 chain) and against about 0.26 implied by the Run 2 ratio (0.28 x 0.933). It did not miss on engagement timing, and once a whiff comes the row almost always leaves lost. The slant also carries the searching agent upwind faster than the design's exact-heading figures (leg 3 at d_along about 22 -> 5 instead of 28 -> 14), which leaves less of leg 3 inside the cones' along range.
- Section 2 (E) is right for two thirds of the stranded rows. The rest come from a hold that forms a few steps after its last whiff. Of the quantities examined, only the value sign of the last whiffed odour separates the stranded silences from the tracking silences on these counts. That is the design's (b4), new state that was not taken.

## 5. What the owner can decide

Listed neutrally. Nothing is adopted, and the H17 Run 2 verdict (stopped at the bench by the (h2) stop rule, no bar decision) stands whichever option is chosen.

- **Close H17.** Run 1 and Run 2 both stopped at their benches and nothing was adopted. Cold search from the stranded state stays a recorded limit, with this measurement on record. The registered order would then continue: adaptive presence (proposed), then H20 Stage B.
- **A Run 3 design.** It would need its own registration, predictions and bench on new seeds before any task seed. Numbers measured on these bench seeds cannot serve as the Run 3 bench. It would have to register at least one of the following, with its reason stated in advance:
  - (i) **A re-signed T3 (a) bar** in H14 format, anchored to the measured effect. At the bench's DP of -0.0650 (sd 0.2465), the pass probability is 0.2287 at -0.05, 0.5272 at -0.04 and 0.8104 at -0.03. It is 0.5 at a bar of -0.0408 (reference arithmetic, diag:279). The re-sign would also have to say what the smaller bar still guards.
  - (ii) **A change to the stop-rule or bar structure.** The (h2) stop rule reads the same quantity as M3 (a). Run 2 section 7 put the chance of clearing both at about 0.38.
  - (iii) **A mechanism change on the side of the first leg.** The measured counts it would rest on are these. Among the stranded rows engaged by step 399, leg 1 toward the pair whiffs 13/19 and away 5/65, and leg 1 is on the flee side in 65/84. In (c1), leg 1 toward whiffs 184/194 and away 47/206. This is the design's rejected option (iii) of section 3.2. Its gain was inferred there and is now partly measured. The design would have to state whether reading flee_side after the release needs a relaxation, and what the change does in T1, W1 and T3a, where flee_side is a random constant.
  - (iv) **An own-state separator.** `since` - q does not separate the two kinds of silence: it is non-zero in 35/105 stranded rows, 4/16 T1 bench-seed silences and 2/18 Run 1 tracking silences. The value sign of the last whiffed odour does separate them on these counts: negative in 105/105 stranded rows, none of 16 T1 bench-seed silences and none of Run 1's 18. It would not engage in any of Run 1's 18 tracking silences, whose last whiffs were valued 16 and neutral 2; rows 77 and 251 are mechanisms (A) and (D) with a neutral whiff. But it is new state beyond q (design section 3.1 (b4)). It would need its own H14-format relaxation, and it would never engage in T1, W1, T3a or (c1) at 0/0.
  - (v) **The negative-plume recapture cost.** After a first negative whiff, 6 of 10 rows re-formed a negative hold and 5 stranded again; only 1 of the 18 whiff rows stayed lost. The 29 stranded rows released at step 414 or later cannot engage at S 250 within 600 steps, under any leg rule.

## 6. What this does not establish

The correlation between cast side and flee side is measured, not explained. The bar figures in (A7) and section 5 are reference arithmetic. The tasks were not run, and no development or evaluation seed was used.
