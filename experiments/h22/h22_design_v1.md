## 1. Hypothesis

**H22: while the agent holds a non-negative odour whose value is lower than the highest value in its own value read-out, that odour's whiffs do not drive the upwind surge; the agent keeps casting while it holds it, and so samples the air it would otherwise not reach. With this rule, in the H21 task, rows whose first hold is the zero-valued odour are revised to the valued odour, and the source the agent stays at follows the value in more rows than with the H21 maintain agent.**

Two statements, judged separately: (i) the rule makes an agent that holds the zero-valued odour at the zero-valued source leave it and take the valued odour (mechanism bench, section 4, M4); (ii) this raises the dwell-majority P(V) over the H21 maintain agent on the same rows without adding lost rows (task, sections 5 to 7, M1 to M3, M5, M6).

## 2. The measured reason, and why this lever (diagnosis from the record; no code was run for it)

Sources: H21 report doc d4fd6c39f7f81e1ee (section 5 and Appendix A), record:h21-result; H20 Run 2 diagnosis doc d73ddadcfb33103cd (record:h20-run2-diagnosis); the link check (record:h20-linkcheck-result); record:h20-post-bench-diagnosis-result; the code (ph11.World2.sense, ph14.Agent3.act, ph16.Agent4.act, ph19.Agent6.act).

**Measured (H21 evaluation, seeds 1725/1825):**
- The loss is in the neutral-first rows. P(V) = 0.70 x 0.971 + 0.30 x 0.233 = 0.750: 280 valued-first rows convert at 272/280; 120 neutral-first rows convert at 28/120; of the 100 non-V rows 92 are neutral-first. The first selection is identical with and without the gate (280 / 120, the same rows).
- Revision is the same with and without the gate: 53 of 120 neutral-first rows later hold the valued odour, in both arms (the neutral-hold phase is identical until the revision). 53 revised, 28 in V: at least 25 rows revised too late to win the dwell majority.
- A revision is possible whenever valued input arrives: with a neutral hold, gate_k = 1 for the valued odour (v_k > v_h) and the gain multiplies its response by 3; the H21 bench (b) revised 400/400 rows under valued-only input at p 0.30, bitwise as the Run 2 agent. So a neutral-first row that does not revise is, by the code path, a row in which valued input did not arrive, or arrived too sparsely, during the neutral hold. (Inference from the code path and the bench; the valued whiffs received during each row's neutral hold were not logged in H21.)
- Where the valued input can arrive (code, ph11.World2.sense; section 5 geometry): a whiff from source k arrives only inside its cone (0 < d_along < 25, |d_cross| < 1.5 + 0.25 d_along) or within 3.0 of source k. The sources are 10 apart crosswind. The valued cone reaches the neutral axis only at d_along > 34, beyond LMAX 25: **on the neutral axis and at the neutral source no valued whiff can arrive at all.** The cones overlap only from 14 downwind; the start is at 20.
- What steers while the neutral odour is held (code, ph16.Agent4.act, unchanged in Agent6): hit = whiffs of the held odour only; a valued whiff steers nothing until it has changed the hold. H19 (a) applies only with nothing held (4.7 percent of steps in maintain). So an agent holding the neutral odour surges up the neutral axis, out of the overlap, into the only region where the valued plume cannot reach it.
- Timing (measured medians, arithmetic not measured): the first hold forms at step 12; from 20 downwind the agent leaves the overlap (d_along < 14) after about 6 units of upwind travel, roughly 10 steps at 0.6 per step; each plume delivers about 0.057 to 0.094 whiffs per step there; expected valued whiffs before the overlap is left 0.6 to 0.9, P(at least one) about 0.45 to 0.6. This is consistent with the measured revision 0.442; it is a mechanism interpretation, not a measured cause.
- The Run 2 diagnosis measured the same state from the other side: in the 77 N rows that lost a valued hold to the neutral odour, valued whiffs were 1.7 per row afterwards against 9.1 neutral; 86 of 103 neutral-hold endings happened after the neutral source had been reached.
- What an agent does that does not surge on the neutral odour (the known-answer arm, identity fixed to the valued odour, neutral whiffs ignored by navigation): P(V) 380/400 = 0.950, although it reached the neutral source first in 183 rows; at least 163 of those 183 rows (0.89) still ended at the valued source. **Leaving the neutral plume and casting is enough to find the valued plume from this geometry.**
- Dwell is not parking: dwell medians are 21.0 / 3.5 steps of 600 in maintain; the agent loops through a source (surge, overshoot, cast, re-entry). The neutral-held loops stay in the neutral plume because each neutral whiff restarts the surge.

**Reading (a mechanism interpretation, to be tested by the bench, section 4 (d)):** non-revision is a sampling failure produced by navigation, not a failure of the circuit to revise and not a failure of the first selection alone. What restarts the surge while the neutral odour is held is the neutral whiff; that is the one signal this design changes.

**Why this lever and not the first selection.** The first-selection lever (make the first hold valued) is bounded before it starts: on the Stage A bench input, 77 of 400 rows formed an unbiased hold before the biased channel's first whiff (record:h20-post-bench-diagnosis-result; ceiling 0.8075 for the tested G on that input, not transferred here as a number, used as an indication only). Even a perfect first selection up to that bound gives 0.8075 x 0.971 + 0.1925 x 0.233 = 0.829 by the H21 arithmetic, against the known-answer ceiling 0.950. And it would not remove the trap: a withheld first hold means nothing held, and with nothing held H19 (a) surges on any non-negative whiff, the neutral one included, so the agent is carried up the neutral axis in the same way. What the first-selection lever would need: a rule that withholds the circuit input of a zero-valued odour for some time or until a valued whiff (a waiting rule, which uses time since the start, a new state), its own bench against the arrival order, and still an answer to the navigation question for the rows where the neutral plume is met first. The navigation lever acts on all 120 neutral-first rows, including the arrival-order rows, and its ceiling is the known-answer arm's.

**Alternatives not chosen (one line each):**
- Value-dependent hold persistence or release of a zero-valued hold: at the neutral source the only input is the neutral odour, so a released circuit re-selects it and H19 (a) keeps surging on it with nothing held; nothing in the record says release is missing (bench (b): a neutral hold is revised 400/400 once valued input arrives). Predicted near-null.
- A stronger G: acts only on valued whiffs that arrive; it changes neither the arrival order nor the missing valued input; G 2 is fixed since Run 2.
- A crosswind or position term: uses the source or axis position; excluded by the owner's classification rule.
- Surge only on a positive value (v_h > 0, without the comparison to the read-out): simpler and closer to the fly reading, but it stops approach to a held odour in every world where all values are 0, which changes the conditions H19 (a) was adopted under (neutral valence) and breaks the 0/0 identity; kept as the owner's alternative (section 12).
- The first selection: above.

**Fly grounding (motivation, cited from the literature as recalled, not re-checked in this session).** In the fly the upwind run on an odour is value-dependent: an attractive odour evokes an upwind surge and its loss evokes casting (Alvarez-Salvado et al. 2018, eLife, already cited at Phase 6.1); mushroom body output neurons carrying a learned valence drive wind-oriented movement, upwind for an appetitive memory (Aso et al. 2023, eLife, 'Neural circuit mechanisms for transforming learned olfactory valences into wind-oriented movement'). An odour without value gives no reason to surge. The relative condition used here (lower than the highest value in the read-out, rather than 'not positive') is the model's scoping choice so that equal values stay an identity; it is not a fly finding.

## 3. The change: one rule, one computational effect, boundaries stated

**Rule.** Let h be the odour held after this step's selection (the h navigation already uses), v_k the value the agent reads for odour k (Agent3.chan_valence; the supplied `known` array here) and v_max = max over k of v_k (the agent's two channels, the same read-out the gain and the gate already use every step). Define

  surge_ok = NOT (h >= 0 and 0 <= v_h < v_max);   nav = (hit AND surge_ok) OR (h < 0 AND any non-negative whiff),

where hit = whiffs of the held odour (unchanged) and the second term is H19 (a) (unchanged). nav is what resets the cast clock (since) and selects the upwind target. **Nothing else changes:** the silence timer that times out the hold still counts hits of the held odour (hit, not nav), so the hold's own clock is unchanged; the selection circuit, its input y'' (gain and gate), the evidence release, the timeout, the return cast and its parameters, the ring, the flee, the learning module (not called) are untouched.

**Effect.** While a lower-valued non-negative odour is held, its whiffs do not restart the upwind surge; the return cast runs on (side flipped every 30 steps, offset a triangle wave up to MAXOFF), so the agent leaves that plume and samples crosswind and downwind. A valued whiff met while casting reaches the circuit as before (gate 1, gain 3) and can revise the hold; once the valued odour is held, surge_ok = 1 and the agent surges on it; the gate then keeps it (H21).

**What the rule does NOT do.** With equal values (0/0, +1/+1) v_h = v_max: the agent IS the H21 maintain agent (Agent6). With nothing held the rule is inert (H19 (a) untouched). With the valued odour held v_h = v_max: inert. **A negative held odour (v_h < 0) is excluded by construction (0 <= v_h):** the flee target overrides as before and the cast clock is driven as before, so at +1/-1 the agent IS Agent6 = the Run 2 agent (M3 ii) and avoidance is not re-tested. The rule does not change which odour is held on any step on which the agent is in the same place with the same whiffs; it changes where the agent goes.

**Classification (owner's rule at the H20 Stage A close).** The rule uses the agent's own value read-out and its own hold state: no source or axis position, no sensory history. **One property to confirm knowingly:** v_max includes the value of an odour the agent is not smelling. That value is part of the agent's own read-out (the MB weights for its odour codes, read every step by the gain and the gate), not a sensory history; but it means the rule acts on the fact that a higher-valued odour is in the agent's repertoire, not that it is present. In the H21 task both odours are always present. A world where the higher-valued odour is absent (the agent would not surge on a held zero-valued odour it could otherwise track) is not tested and bounds any adoption (section 11).

**Implementation boundary.** One subclass Agent7 of ph19.Agent6 (the H21 maintain agent: Agent4 + the gate, G 2, rule on), overriding act only where nav is formed; a `resample` flag, so that resample=False is the same class with surge_ok = 1 (M3 i checks it equals ph19.Agent6 bitwise). No adopted module edited.

## 5. The task, stated in full (the H21 task = the H20 Run 2 task, unchanged)

**World.** Two odour sources at the same downwind coordinate, SEP = 10 units apart crosswind, in the 160 x 160 translated arena of the H16 Run 2 and H15 Run 2 worlds (walls at least 68 units from either source; a wall reflects the heading and triggers the agent's bump reflex, which flips the flee side and the cast side). Plume model as everywhere since Phase 7.3: a whiff from source k arrives with probability 0.30 * exp(-d_along / 12) per step inside the cone 0 < d_along < 25 (LMAX) and |d_cross| < 1.5 + 0.25 * d_along, and also within 3.0 of the source; independent draws per source per step. Speed 0.6 per step; a source is reached when the agent is within HIT_R = 3.0 of it. Wind sensed every step (upwind = 180 degrees). Start: on the midline between the two axes, DOWN = 20 units downwind of the sources, heading uniform at random; there the cone half-width is 6.5 against 5 to each axis, so the start is inside both cones and each plume delivers a whiff with probability 0.057 per step. The two cones overlap from 14 units downwind onward; the valued cone never reaches the neutral axis inside LMAX. No relocation; one persistent trial per row; 600 steps.

**Balance, crossed 2 x 2.** Which odour code (A or B) carries the positive value, and on which side (+y or -y) the valued source sits: 100 rows per cell, 400 rows, assigned by a permutation seeded from the world seed + 10000 (cell // 2 = the valued odour's index; even cells put the valued source at +y). The initial cast side is drawn per row from a generator seeded with the agent seed + 20000 (decision:h20-amendment-initial-cast-side), the same draw in every arm. All arms share the world seed, the agent seed, the row index and the cast draw; matched rows are the same world and the same agent noise stream.

**Agent constants (adopted, unchanged).** Upstream stage: Heeger form, exponent 1.5, sigma 0.05, Rmax 1.8, cross-channel k 0.8, tau 2. Selection circuit: 2 units, theta 1.0, k 12, self-excitation g 2, w_i 1, tau 10, pool tau 2, noise 0.01, S_MAX 5; a hold is a unit above 1.0 when exactly one is; releases: silence > RESET_AFTER = 40 steps of no hit of the held odour, and evidence y''[other] - y''[held] > MARGIN = 0.2, each delivering a one-step drive of 10 into the pool. Navigation: surge upwind on nav, return cast otherwise (CAST_PERIOD 30, MAXOFF 170, triangle-wave offset saturating at 141.7 steps), turn = 0.6 x heading error clipped at 40 degrees plus noise 6.0 degrees; H19 (a): with nothing held, any whiff of an odour whose value is not negative counts for navigation; the ring (RingExact, tau 1, sigma 0.5, n 16, fed the rotation made) estimates the heading; the flee side is drawn per row (90 or 270 degrees) and taken while a negative odour is held. Values supplied per odour (+1 / 0 / -1) through the `known` array; the learning module is not called. Gain: y'_k = y_k (1 + 2 max(v_k, 0)) (G 2). **The H21 gate on in every arm with G 2** (adopted within exactly these conditions: supplied value, +1/0, G 2, C0, dwell majority, learning off; decision:h21-gate-adopted-within-tested-conditions). The H22 rule of section 3 is applied in the arms marked rule on.

**Main measure, fixed:** dwell_k = steps within 3.0 of source k over the 600 steps; choice = the source with the larger dwell; tie (equal dwell, including 0 = 0) = no choice. Every row is exactly one of V, N, 0 (tie); all three counts printed per arm and per cell; no row dropped.

**Secondary and diagnostics, reported (as H21, plus):** first source reached; first hold (odour, step); hold changes; odour held at the first reach; segment table; agreement of the majority with the first hold; dwell medians; dwell at each source split by the odour held (valued / neutral / nothing); steps with nothing held; hold endings by flag and by the held unit's s the step before; timeout firings and the holds they ended; revision rate of neutral-first rows and the step of revision (median); input-range records (an arm above 1 percent on the clip flagged); no whiff of either plume in the last third; no navigation hit in the last third; wall contacts per row; paired flips against maintain on the same rows. **Added for H22:** per row the first step on which the rule withheld a surge (none if never); the number of withheld (row, step); valued whiffs per row received while the neutral odour was held; the cast clock's maximum while the neutral odour was held; P(V | first hold valued) and P(V | first hold neutral) per arm (the two terms of the arithmetic in section 10).

## 11. What this design does not test

Learning (H20 Stage B); the integrated environment (Stage C); values other than +1/0 (e.g. +1/+0.5, where the rule would also withhold the surge on the lower positive odour); **a world where the higher-valued odour is absent** (the cost of the rule: the agent would not surge on a held zero-valued odour); any G other than 2; any geometry other than C0; the first selection (identical by construction); the rule without the gate (no resample arm without the gate; the gate is adopted in these conditions); the timeout (a recorded limit, record:silence-timeout-chain-result); H19 (a) with nothing held (untouched); avoidance with the rule acting on a negative odour (excluded by construction); a crosswind or position term.
