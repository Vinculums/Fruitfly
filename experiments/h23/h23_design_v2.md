## 1. Hypothesis

**H23: the agent's upwind surge follows the highest-valued non-negative odour in its own value read-out. While it holds that odour, the held odour's whiffs drive the surge (as now); otherwise (nothing held, or a lower-valued non-negative odour held) only whiffs of a top-valued non-negative odour drive it, and whiffs of lower-valued odours never do. With this rule, in the H21 task, the source the agent stays at follows the value in more rows than with the H21 maintain agent, near the known-answer ceiling, without adding lost rows.**

Two statements, judged separately: (i) implementation, on constructed states before the task (section 4, M4): the rule steers only on a top-valued odour whatever is held; identities hold; (ii) task (sections 5 to 7): dwell-majority P(V) over the maintain agent on the same rows, near the known-answer arm, no added lost rows (M1 to M3, M5, M6).

**Registered REPORTED property (not a gate; owner, 2026-09-23):** whether the hold comes to agree with navigation from the task-like neutral-hold start: bench (b), with its bar (paired DP of holding the valued odour at step 600, Agent8 minus Agent6, bootstrap lower bound > 0), its prediction (section 10) and its pass probabilities (section 7, M4) kept as written. Its PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE label is printed and reported; it does not decide M4 and does not stop the task.

**Reading (interpretation, as recorded at the H22 closure):** the first selection and every revision are decided from the empty state, where H19 (a) surges on any non-negative whiff; with the neutral plume nearer, the neutral odour re-takes the hold. The H21 gate protects a valued hold; nothing protects the empty state. The H22 rule acted only on the held-neutral state and left the empty state's navigation (H19 (a)) as it was.

**What the known-answer arm shows (H21 evaluation, seeds 1725/1825):** with navigation driven by valued whiffs only, P(V) 380/400 = 0.950 [0.924, 0.967], cells 0.91 / 0.97 / 0.97 / 0.95 (Run 2: 377/400 = 0.943, cells 0.93 to 0.96); no whiff of either plume in the last third 2 of 400; wall contacts 0.00. In H22 this arm was used as an analogy for a different navigation law; here it is not an analogy (section 3: the H23 rule produces this arm's navigation law exactly at +1/0), and the bench predictions (section 10) are computed from the diagnosis's rates, not from this arm.

**Why this lever.** The diagnosis located the loss in the step 'released -> valued held', decided in the empty state and driven by navigation that returns to the neutral plume. Letting navigation follow the top-valued odour in the empty state and in the held-lower state removes both the surge up the neutral plume (H19 (a) on neutral whiffs) and the blindness to valued whiffs while the neutral odour is held (H22 withheld the neutral surge but did not steer on valued whiffs). It does not change the circuit, so whether the circuit's hold follows is measured, not assumed (bench (b)).

**Alternatives not chosen (one line each):** the H21 gate extended to the empty state (a selection change; the owner chose value-filtered navigation, decision:h23-open-design); a longer bench window alone (diagnosis: 0.328 at 600, upper bound 0.375, below any useful bar); resample-loop geometry (cast period or amplitude; changes adopted navigation parameters in every world, not only when values differ); an absolute rule 'surge only on v > 0' (breaks the 0/0 identity and the conditions H19 (a) was adopted under).

**Fly grounding (motivation, cited from the literature as recalled, not re-checked in this session).** Mushroom body output neurons carrying a learned valence drive wind-oriented movement, upwind for an appetitive memory (Aso et al. 2023, eLife, 'Neural circuit mechanisms for transforming learned olfactory valences into wind-oriented movement'); an attractive odour evokes an upwind surge and its loss evokes casting (Alvarez-Salvado et al. 2018, eLife). In that reading the value of what is smelled, not a held identity, gates the upwind run. The relative condition (top-valued in the read-out, not 'positive') is the model's scoping choice so that equal values stay an identity; it is not a fly finding.

## 3. The change: one rule, boundaries stated

**Rule.** Let h be the odour held after this step's selection (the h navigation already uses), v_k the value the agent reads for odour k (Agent3.chan_valence; the supplied `known` array here), v_max = max over k of v_k (the agent's two channels; the same read-out the gain and the gate use every step), top_k = (v_k >= 0 and v_k = v_max) (a top-valued non-negative odour), hit = whiffs of the held odour (unchanged). Then

  nav = hit                                   if h >= 0 and (v_h = v_max or v_h < 0)   (a top-valued odour held; or a negative odour held: unchanged)
  nav = any whiff of an odour k with top_k    otherwise                                (nothing held, or 0 <= v_h < v_max)

nav resets the cast clock (since) and sets the upwind target, as before. **Nothing else changes:** the silence timer that times out the hold still counts hit (whiffs of the held odour, including a held lower-valued one), the flee target for a held negative odour overrides as before, and the selection circuit, its input y'' (gain, gate), the evidence release, the timeout, the return cast and its parameters, the ring and the learning module (not called) are untouched.

**Which lines change (read in the code).** ph19.Agent6.act, line 63: `nav = hit | ((h < 0) & (whiffs & (self.chan_valence() >= 0)).any(1))` (the same line as ph16.Agent4.act line 90, ph17.Agent5.act line 63 and ph14.Agent3.act line 83). One subclass Agent8(ph19.Agent6) in a new file ph21.py overrides act as a copy of Agent6.act (ph19.py lines 44 to 74) in which only that line is replaced, under a flag `filt`:

  v = self.chan_valence(); vmax = v.max(1); vh = v[rows, np.maximum(h, 0)]; top = (v >= 0) & (v == vmax[:, None])
  keep = (h >= 0) & ((vh == vmax) | (vh < 0))
  nav = np.where(keep, hit, (whiffs & top).any(1)) if self.filt else hit | ((h < 0) & (whiffs & (v >= 0)).any(1))

Line 62 (hit) and line 66 (silence counts hit) stay. For measurement Agent8 also exposes `nav6` (the Agent6 expression on the same step) and `differs = nav != nav6`; neither is read by the agent. No adopted module edited.

**Identities, checked by reading Agent6.act.** The rule differs from line 63 only on (row, step) where some non-negative odour is not top-valued, i.e. where two non-negative odours have different values. (1) filt False: line 63 itself. (2) 0/0 and +1/+1: every odour is top-valued; held -> v_h = v_max -> hit; nothing held -> any whiff with v >= 0, which is H19 (a) exactly. (3) +1/-1: v_max = +1; held valued -> hit; held negative -> hit and flee (unchanged); nothing held -> only the +1 whiff qualifies, which is what H19 (a) already does since -1 < 0; the state 0 <= v_h < v_max does not exist. (4) +1/0 with the valued odour held: v_h = v_max -> hit. (5) all-negative values: no odour is top (v >= 0 fails), and H19 (a) admits no whiff either. So at 0/0, +1/+1, +1/-1, and +1/0 while the valued odour is held, Agent8 is Agent6 bitwise (same draws: the rule consumes no random numbers). The float test v_k = v_max is exact for supplied values; with learned values ties are measure-zero and 'top' would be a single odour (outside this design).

**What the rule contains.** (a) The H22 rule as a special case: while a lower-valued non-negative odour is held, its whiffs never drive the surge. (b) New effect 1: while a lower-valued odour is held, a top-valued whiff drives the surge (H22 did not steer on it); the held identity no longer gates navigation in that state. (c) New effect 2: in the empty state only top-valued whiffs drive the surge; H19 (a) is narrowed by value (it is unchanged wherever all non-negative values are equal, including the neutral-valence conditions it was adopted under).

**The conceptual change, stated plainly for the owner.** At +1/0 the rule makes nav equal to 'a whiff of the valued odour' in every hold state: valued held -> hit = valued whiffs; neutral held -> top whiffs = valued whiffs; nothing held -> valued whiffs. The flee branch never applies (no negative odour). Hence **at +1/0 the agent's trajectory does not depend on the circuit's state at all**: it is the same function of whiffs and noise as the known-answer arm's (ph17.Agent5: h fixed to the valued odour, nav = hit = valued whiffs). The two differ only in the random stream (Agent5 does not step the circuit, whose noise draws come from the same agent generator), so they are two draws of the same navigation law, equal in distribution, not bitwise. Consequences: (1) in this task the known-answer arm's navigation is what the rule produces whenever the valued odour is known; (2) **the task therefore cannot show that selection controls navigation**, and a pass is a statement about value-driven navigation in the full agent, not about selection; (3) the circuit still runs, and whether its hold comes to agree with where navigation goes is a separate, measured property (bench (b); reported in the task). The owner accepted this (decision:h23-open-design).

**What selection still does.** Arbitration at equal values (0/0, +1/+1: only the held odour's whiffs steer); the flee from a held negative odour (+1/-1, avoidance); the hold state that the H21 gate protects and that the evidence release and the timeout act on (the timeout's clock still counts the held odour's whiffs); and, in worlds with two positive unequal values (untested, section 11), nothing for navigation beyond the flee. At +1/0 in this task: nothing for navigation.

**Classification (owner's rule at the H20 Stage A close).** Own value read-out and own hold state only; no source or axis position, no sensory history. Kept from H22 and repeated with its cost: v_max includes the value of an odour the agent is not smelling (part of its read-out, not a sensory history), so the rule acts on the fact that a higher-valued odour is in the repertoire, not that it is present. **A world where the higher-valued odour is absent is untested:** the agent would ignore every whiff of a lower-valued non-negative odour for navigation, held or not, and would cast indefinitely in a world containing only that odour; this bounds any adoption. The +1/+0.5 case (two positive values) is untested.

**Implementation boundary.** Agent8 = ph19.Agent6 + the rule, flag `filt`; filt=False is Agent6 bitwise (M3 i). No adopted module edited; ph20.Agent7 not used.

## 5. The task, stated in full (the H21 task = the H20 Run 2 task, unchanged)

**World.** Two odour sources at the same downwind coordinate, SEP = 10 units apart crosswind, in the 160 x 160 translated arena of the H16 Run 2 and H15 Run 2 worlds (walls at least 68 units from either source; a wall reflects the heading and triggers the agent's bump reflex, which flips the flee side and the cast side). Plume model as everywhere since Phase 7.3: a whiff from source k arrives with probability 0.30 * exp(-d_along / 12) per step inside the cone 0 < d_along < 25 (LMAX) and |d_cross| < 1.5 + 0.25 * d_along, and also within 3.0 of the source; independent draws per source per step. Speed 0.6 per step; a source is reached when the agent is within HIT_R = 3.0 of it. Wind sensed every step (upwind = 180 degrees). Start: on the midline between the two axes, DOWN = 20 units downwind of the sources, heading uniform at random; there the cone half-width is 6.5 against 5 to each axis, so the start is inside both cones and each plume delivers a whiff with probability 0.057 per step. The cones overlap only from 14 units downwind onward; the valued cone never reaches the neutral axis inside LMAX (it would need d_along > 34). No relocation; one persistent trial per row; 600 steps.

**Balance, crossed 2 x 2.** Which odour code (A or B) carries the positive value, and on which side (+y or -y) the valued source sits: 100 rows per cell, 400 rows, assigned by a permutation seeded from the world seed + 10000 (cell // 2 = the valued odour's index; even cells put the valued source at +y). The initial cast side is drawn per row from a generator seeded with the agent seed + 20000 (decision:h20-amendment-initial-cast-side), the same draw in every arm. All arms share the world seed, the agent seed, the row index and the cast draw.

**Agent constants (adopted, unchanged).** Upstream stage: Heeger form, exponent 1.5, sigma 0.05, Rmax 1.8, cross-channel k 0.8, tau 2. Selection circuit: 2 units, theta 1.0, k 12, self-excitation g 2, w_i 1, tau 10, pool tau 2, noise 0.01, S_MAX 5; a hold is a unit above 1.0 when exactly one is; releases: silence > RESET_AFTER = 40 steps of no hit of the held odour, and evidence y''[other] - y''[held] > MARGIN = 0.2, each delivering a one-step drive of 10 into the pool. Navigation: surge upwind on nav, return cast otherwise (CAST_PERIOD 30, MAXOFF 170, triangle-wave offset saturating at 141.7 steps), turn = 0.6 x heading error clipped at 40 degrees plus noise 6.0 degrees; H19 (a) with nothing held; the ring (RingExact, tau 1, sigma 0.5, n 16, fed the rotation made); flee side drawn per row (90 or 270) and taken while a negative odour is held. Values supplied per odour through `known`; the learning module is not called. Gain y'_k = y_k (1 + 2 max(v_k, 0)) (G 2). The H21 gate on in every arm with G 2. The H23 rule of section 3 in the arms marked filter on.

**Main measure, fixed:** dwell_k = steps within 3.0 of source k over the 600 steps; choice = the source with the larger dwell; tie (equal dwell, including 0 = 0) = no choice. Every row is exactly one of V, N, 0 (tie); all three counts printed per arm and per cell; no row dropped.

**Secondary and diagnostics, reported (as H21 and H22, plus):** first source reached; first hold (odour, step); hold changes; segment table; dwell medians; dwell at each source split by the odour held; steps with nothing held; hold endings by flag; timeout firings; revision rate of neutral-first rows and its step; input-range records; no whiff of either plume in the last third; no navigation hit in the last third; wall contacts per row; paired flips against maintain. **Added for H23:** per row the first step on which filter's nav differs from Agent6's expression (`differs`), and the count of such (row, step); rows holding the valued odour at step 600 per arm (whether the hold follows navigation); P(V | first hold valued) and P(V | first hold neutral) per arm; P(V) filter / known-answer.

## 7. Criteria

Denominators: M2 and M6 every assigned row; M1's symmetry clauses the rows that chose. Aggregation: a criterion is PASS if every part passes, FAIL if any part fails, INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. Sampling unit the row; rows share no state (one generator per world and one per agent population, consumed in row order every step; printed). The per-row odour codes do not vary with the seed; results are conditional on that set.

## 11. What this design does not test

That selection controls navigation (at +1/0 it does not, by construction; section 3); learning (H20 Stage B); the integrated environment (Stage C); values other than +1/0, in particular +1/+0.5 (the lower positive odour would never steer) and **a world where the higher-valued odour is absent** (the agent would never surge on the only odour present if it is not top-valued; the rule's cost, bounding any adoption); any G other than 2; any geometry other than C0; the rule without the gate as a separate arm (bitwise the same trajectories at +1/0; the gate's effect on the hold only is reported by bench (a5)); the timeout (a recorded limit, record:silence-timeout-chain-result); a change to the circuit (none is made); avoidance with the rule acting on a negative odour (excluded by construction); the first selection as a lever (it no longer steers at +1/0); a crosswind or position term.
