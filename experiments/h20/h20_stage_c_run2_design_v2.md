**Within (i), why dwell > 0 and not >= 1, 5 or 10.** Dwell > 0 is G3+, the set on which the WIN is already registered (Run 1 section 7 M4(c), confirmed by the owner in decision:h20-stage-c-open point 6); reading the readability there makes the readability and the WIN read the same rows, which is the whole point of the change. Dwell >= 1 equals G3+ on the bench (no row in (0, 1), diag:40) but is a new cut the WIN does not use. Dwell >= 5 or >= 10 would either leave the readability and the WIN on different rows again or move the WIN's rows too (a second change); and >= 10 selects on the readability bar's own number, so 'GM at least 10' would pass by construction and guard nothing. Rejected.

### 3.2 What the chosen reading guards and what it gives up (stated before any run)

**What it guards (read and inferred).**
- The standing rule added at the Phase 7.3 close ('a criterion that compares against a baseline must check the baseline is not pinned at zero') is met on the rows the WIN compares: the floor must dwell at the punisher with a group median of at least 10 per block **on those rows**, and there must be at least 50 of them. A WIN of learned below a floor that does not dwell at the punisher cannot pass.
- The RGM part stays on all of G3 (ph30.py:943), unchanged: the learned arm's median dwell over every punished row must be at most 0.75 of the floor's median over every punished row. It fails safe: where the floor's G3 median is 0 in a resample, the ratio is 0/0 (nan, dropped by np.nanpercentile at ph15.py:184) or x/0 (inf, which cannot pass 'at most 0.75'); it cannot pass on a floor pinned at 0.
- M4(b) stays on all of G3 (ph30.py:942): a learned arm dwelling at the punisher in more than half the punished rows fails it whatever G3+ is.

**What it gives up (read and measured).**
- **G3+ is selected by the floor arm's own outcome** (its last-third punishing dwell). A row where the floor left the punisher for a reason other than learning (on the bench: the H25 release ended its P hold and an R whiff captured it at the reward source, 88/199 rows, diag:49, diag:53-56) is **excluded from the comparison, not counted against the floor.** The WIN and the readability then speak only for the rows where the no-learning agent ends at the punisher.
- **Losses in the excluded rows are invisible to the WIN.** In a row where the floor dwells 0, the learned arm can only tie (0) or lose (above 0). A learned arm that dwelt at the punisher where the floor did not would not be seen by the WIN on G3+; it is seen only by M4(b) and the RGM (medians over G3) and by the reported all-G3 WIN. On the bench the learned arm's last-third punishing dwell is 0 in all 199 G3 rows (diag:204), so no such loss exists there.
- **The rows are not a random half.** 103 of the 111 G3+ rows never sensed an R whiff in 5400 steps in the floor arm (diag:67; report (A3)); G3+ is enriched for world draws in which the reward plume is hard to reach from the punisher. The direction of any bias this gives the WIN is not measured; M5 (reach after avoidance) is read on all of G3, not on G3+.
- **Reported beside, no bar (so that nothing given up is hidden):** (1) the floor's GM over all G3, Run 1's reading, with its interval and zero count; (2) the WIN over all G3 with wins / ties / losses and its tie fraction, labelled UNREADABLE (ties) by the tie rule when above 0.20; (3) every G3 row outside G3+ with its last-third end state in ph30b's classes (i) at the reward source, (ii) at the punisher uncounted, (iii) lost, (iv) at a wall, (v) other (ph30b.py (A2)), and the learned arm's last-third punishing dwell in those rows (a loss there is a row with learned dwell above 0); (4) option (ii): H15 Run 2's agent without learning as a floor (its GM over G3, the RGM learned / it over G3, the WIN on its own dwell > 0 rows).

**The tie rule, exactly.** The H15 Run 2 tie rule (ph15.py:197-200; ph30.py:426-428: a WIN whose exact ties, a == b, exceed 0.20 of its rows is UNREADABLE) is **not touched**: its threshold, its counting and its consequence are unchanged wherever a WIN is read. The criterion WIN is read on G3+, as Run 1 registered and the owner confirmed (decision:h20-stage-c-open point 6); this re-sign does not move it. Ties are still counted on G3+ (there they can come only from equal positive dwell; 0 on the bench, diag:201). The WIN on all of G3, Run 2's Q3(c) form (ph15.py:272), is reported with its ties counted and is UNREADABLE by the same rule whenever they exceed 0.20 (44.2 percent on the bench); it is not used as a criterion and the rule is not waived for it. What the re-sign moves is only the set of rows on which the floor's readability GM is computed (G3 -> G3+) and the stop rule that reads it at the bench. H15 Run 2's Q3(c) and its UNREADABLE WIN (run2:192) are not re-judged.

**What M4(c) then claims.** 'In the P-start rows punished at least once, the learned agent's median last-third dwell at the punisher is at most 0.75 of the no-learning agent's (all punished rows), and among the punished rows in which the no-learning agent ends at the punisher (G3+, at least 50 rows, where it dwells a median of at least 10 steps per block), the learned agent dwells less than it in at least 60 percent of rows, with at most 20 percent exact ties.' Under option (ii) as a criterion the claim would instead be 'dwells less than H15 Run 2's agent without learning', a contrast that mixes the agent difference (the gain, gate, filter and counter, inert at 0/0 by the diagnosis's reading, and the H25 release, which is not) with learning; that is why (ii) is reported and not the criterion.

**Pass probability of M4 under (iii) (measured on the bench rows, quoted; inferred for new seeds).** M4(a): -0.886 [-0.892, -0.800] against -0.5, above 0.99. M4(b): 0.000 [0.000, 0.000] against 1.0, above 0.99. M4(c): readability on G3+ 1.000, RGM 1.000, WIN 1.000 on resampled bench rows (diag:231). **M4 about 0.98 to 0.99** on a new world draw, the allowance being for what the resampling cannot see (section 10): the floor's share of G3 at the punisher (0.558 on the bench) and its zero share (0.442) on another draw. The G3+ count guard binds only if fewer than 50 of about 199 G3 rows end at the punisher (a share under 0.25; at the bench share the count is 111, binomial sd about 7, so under 50 is about 8.7 sd away); the RGM on G3 becomes INCONCLUSIVE only if nearly every resample has a floor median of 0, which needs a zero share of about 0.6 or more (0.442 on the bench; about 0.27 for Agent3 in H15 Run 2, the tie share of run2:192, where the intact arm sits at 0 in almost every G3 row).
