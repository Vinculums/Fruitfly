## 2. What is already known (by code, not assumed; checked in R0)

- At +1/-1 Agent8 == Agent6 bitwise (H23 M3 (ii)): the only non-negative odour is the valued one, so the filter's `top` and Agent6's `v >= 0` select the same whiffs. Hence Agent10 == Agent10g at +1/-1 too (the same mixin on bitwise-equal bases). R3's two comparisons are then one comparison; the run checks this and says so.
- The flee target (ph19.Agent6.act / ph21.Agent8.act: `tgt = flee_side where val < 0`) is set while the negative odour is HELD. The base never ends a silent hold on the timeout (H25 bench (b): 0/400 released), so in the base a negative hold that stops being whiffed keeps the flee target until an evidence release. With the release it ends 47 steps after the last whiff (H25 (b), valued and neutral holds; predicted here for the negative hold), and the flee target ends with it.

## 3. Readings, fixed before the run (three outcomes each)

Wilson 95 percent intervals for proportions; paired percentile bootstrap over rows for differences, 5000 resamples, seed 20261033 (ph24's, reused); one run; a group under 50 rows is unreadable. Values +1/-1 (the valued odour +1, the other -1), G 2, gate on, C0, 400 rows x 600 steps unless stated.

Also printed: Agent8 == Agent6 and Agent10 == Agent10g bitwise at +1/-1. Outcomes: **'identity holds'** (separation True in both pairs and both +1/-1 equalities True); **'identity holds, cross-equality fails'** (separation True, but Agent8 != Agent6 or Agent10 != Agent10g at +1/-1: not clean, it contradicts a recorded identity); **'identity broken'** (separation False in either pair: not clean).
- **R1 the H21 constructed-state priority check, re-run with the release on** (ph19.priority_check6's protocol: ph16.Still stub, 50 rows, agent generator 11, channel 1 = the negative odour held by construction (s 2.0), both channels presented on every step, G 2). Two lengths: 20 steps (the recorded protocol) and 200 steps (past RESET_AFTER + 7). Arms Agent10 vs Agent8, Agent10g vs Agent6. Statistic: on every negative-held step the target is the flee side, and the target equals the base's on every step. Outcomes: **'priority kept, identical'** (flee on every negative-held step, target and hold equal to the base's on every step); **'priority kept, differs'** (flee on every negative-held step, but the hold or target differs from the base's somewhere: not clean, reported with counts); **'priority violated'** (a negative-held step without the flee target: not clean).
- **R2 constructed negative hold, then silence** (ph24.stub, bench seeds 20261031/20261032, 400 rows, 200 steps, no input, channel 1 held s 2.0, values +1/-1), Agent10 and Agent10g against Agent8 / Agent6. Statistic per row: the hold ends at step 47 exactly (the valued and neutral holds' step in H25 (b)), both units <= 1.0 at the end step and none above 1.0 afterwards, the target is the flee side on every held step, and the negative odour is not held on any step from the end step on, so the flee assignment (`tgt = flee_side where val < 0`) is inactive by code: the flee target ends with the release. (Amendment before the recorded run, stated: the first wording, 'the target is not the flee side from the end step on', was found in an operation smoke test to fail on a numeric coincidence of the cast formula: with nothing held the cast target at since 75 is 180 + 170 x 75/141.7 = 270, equal to a flee side. Outcomes: **'released as valued holds'** (every row exact at 47, lower bound >= 0.95); **'released, other step'** (every row released within the registered <= 49 with the flee target ending with it, but not all at 47: clean for adoption, the step reported); **'not released'** (any row not released by step 49, or a flee target outliving the hold, or a unit above 1.0 after the end: not clean). Reported beside it: what the agent does next (in the stub, nothing held; its target is the cast target, H19 (a)), the bases' hold at step 199 (expected kept 400/400).
- **R3 the +1/-1 task arm** (World7, 1805/1905, 400 rows x 600 steps). Agent10 vs Agent8: P(V) (dwell majority) with its interval; mean dwell at the negative source (steps within 3.0); flee violations = (row, step) with the negative odour held and the target not the flee side (must be 0 in both); wall contacts per row; lost rows (no whiff of either plume in the last third). Label **'avoidance kept'** iff (i) flee violations 0 in both arms, (ii) the paired dwell-at-negative difference Agent10 - Agent8 has bootstrap upper bound <= +2.0 steps, and (iii) the paired DP = P(V) Agent10 - Agent8 has bootstrap lower bound >= -0.05 (the owner's 'P(V) lower bound within 0.05 of Agent8's', read as the project's allowed-gap convention; the literal reading, Agent10's Wilson lower bound >= Agent8's P(V) - 0.05, is printed beside it and any disagreement is reported); **'avoidance changed'** if any of (i)-(iii) fails; **'unreadable'** if the reference arm is invalid (ties above 0.20 in either arm). Reported beside it: negative holds formed and ended (by the drive / by evidence), the hold state on the step after a negative hold ends by the drive (nothing held; re-held negative within 60 steps; valued held within 60 steps), steps with the flee target, lost rows and contacts paired.

**Clean** = R0 'identity holds', R1 'priority kept, identical', R2 'released as valued holds' or 'released, other step', R3 'avoidance kept' (Agent10 vs Agent8). ## 6. Predictions, on record

R0 'identity holds' (by the H25 identity (b) argument, which does not depend on the values). R1 'priority kept, identical': both channels are whiffed on every step, so the negative hold's silence counter is reset by every hit and the timeout never fires; any change of hold comes from the evidence release, identical in both. R2 'released as valued holds' (47): the gate is inert while a negative odour is held (0 <= v_k < v_h is never true for v_h = -1) and there is no input, so the circuit is the valued-hold stub's. R3 'avoidance kept' more likely than not: navigation never steers on a negative whiff (nav keeps only non-negative whiffs when nothing is held), so ending a silent negative hold removes a flee target on steps where the agent is already outside the negative plume; P(V) near the recorded +1/-1 identity arms (H21 363/400 = 0.908, H23 0.908); a small rise in dwell at the negative source is possible (the cast resumes 47 steps after the last negative whiff instead of the flee continuing), and I do not predict its size.
