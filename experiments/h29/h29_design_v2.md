### 2.2 Why the window is fixed there, by construction (read)

- The counter c_k starts at N_hi - P = 240 (ph28.py:97), is zeroed on a whiff of k and incremented otherwise (ph23.py:83); k is present while c_k < N_hi = 300 or k is held (ph23.py:85). After the last valued whiff L the valued odour is present on L to L + 299 whatever happens in between: nothing else enters presence.
- A silent valued hold ends 48 steps after its odour's last whiff (the H25 release, ph24.py:47-63, as N2 at non-negative values, ph30.py:117-136; H25: 'T3b 80/80 and 173/173 rows ... 48'; H26 eval 'ended 180 at origin + 48/48/48'), so on L + 48 to L + 299 the valued odour is present only by the counter.
- At +1/0 nav depends on the valued presence only (H26 design 2.3): while the valued odour is present, v_max = +1 and a neutral whiff with nothing or the neutral odour held does not steer (ph23.py:88-92). So the agent cannot steer on the neutral odour on L + 1 to L + 299. **That is the limit: a fixed 300-step blindness after the last valued whiff, whether the valued plume has gone (T3b) or the agent is only between two passes (T1).**

### 2.3 Where the neutral plume is met after a loss: the cast loop (read, then measured)

- `since` is zeroed on nav and incremented otherwise (ph23.py:95). The cast target is upwind plus a side offset MAXOFF (1 - |(since / SAT) mod 2 - 1|) (ph23.py:97-98), MAXOFF 170 degrees (ph9.py:40), SAT = MAXOFF / CAST_GROW = 141.7 steps (ph12.py:29), side alternating every CAST_PERIOD 30 steps (ph9.py:40). So after the last nav the offset rises from 0 (upwind) to 170 degrees (nearly downwind) at since 141.7 and returns to 0 at since 283.3: one loop, repeated. **Read.**
- The last valued whiff L steers (the valued odour is present and top, ph23.py:92), so since = t - L during the blind window. The agent therefore comes back downwind through the plumes in the middle of each loop. **Read, inferred.**
- **Measured where that happens.** In T3b the first neutral whiff at or after L + 60 comes at L + 173/181/210 (Agent11 (N 60)); the first at or after L + 300 at L + 471/476/479 (Agent14), about one loop (283) later. In T3a (the loss at the valued source from step 0; the sweep, where N sets the prior only): first neutral surge 176/186/212 for N 60 to 150, **213/235/243 at N 200**, 476/479/507 at N 300 and 450. So the first loop's neutral encounters begin at about 170 steps after the last nav and reach past 200 in most T3a rows (213/235/243 at N 200); the second loop's begin at about 470; from 300 to about 470 three quarters of the rows have none (the lower quartiles 471 and 476). **Measured** (quartiles). Where the first loop's pass ends between about 245 and 300 is not on record.
- Consequence: a window of 300 misses the first loop in almost every row and waits for the second; a window that closes inside the first loop's pass (about 200 to 240) catches it; a window that closes after the pass has ended behaves like 300 (where the pass ends, between about 245 and 300, is not on record). **Inferred.**
- What the agent does in the blind steps is not measured. During them it never surges (nav only on a valued whiff, and none comes), which is the never-surging agent of W1 (Agent10 / Agent8): in the absent-odour check that agent casts past the source and drifts upwind (ends upwind of the source, 111 rows at the wall at 1800 steps; record:absent-odour-check-result). Whether the T3b agent drifts upwind of both sources by L + 300, which would explain the 173 rows with no neutral surge before step 600, was **not on record** at v1 (section 12 point 1 measured it: at L + 300, 381/385 = 0.990 of the agents are upwind of both sources, F2; record:t3b-diagnosis-result).

### 2.5 Can any own-state rule tell a T1 tracking silence from a T3b loss? No, by construction (read, measured identity)

- The Lost world is World7 until t0 (ph23.py:112-119: `Lost.sense` returns World7's draw while `on` is False; ph24.py:133 sets `on` from t0 150). From t0 the valued column is masked while every draw, the neutral column and the wind stay those of the unmasked twin at the same position (ph24.py:136-141, 'draws_equal'). Checked on every task and bench run: 'T3b == T1 on steps 0-149' and 'masked draws == World7 twin' True (H26 eval M3; H26 bench (f)). **Read, measured identity.**
- Hence, for row r, let F_r be the first step at or after t0 on which the T1 twin senses the valued odour. On every step before F_r the T3b row and its T1 twin receive the same whiffs and the same wind and hold the same internal state, **whatever rule computes presence from the agent's own history** (counters, burst records, hold state, cast phase, neutral-whiff counts). A rule that opens the filter in the T3b row at step s < F_r opens it in the T1 twin at s too. **Inferred (exact, from the construction).**
- So the T3b gain of any own-state rule decomposes into (i) openings before F_r, which are the same event as a T1 opening inside a tracking silence of the same row (a `differs8` step if a neutral whiff steers there), and (ii) openings after F_r, where T1 has already reset. A rule can only choose which silences to open, by what it has seen; the record carries no feature of the history before a silence that predicts its length: the long T1 silences start right after a source visit (16/18 with `since` 1, H17 (A5)), and T3b's loss IS such a silence (L 37/41/122 are the last whiffs of the approach or of the dwell at the valued source, H26 bench). **Inferred.**
- **Plainly: the record implies that no rule on the agent's own state can shorten the window after a loss without shortening it equally in the T1 silence the lost row shares with its twin.** The only freedom is the window's length (fixed or history-dependent), traded between the T1 rows whose first return after L + W meets the neutral plume first and the T3b rows whose first return after L + W meets it at all. The trade's T1 side is measured at W 200 and 300 (2.4); its T3b side is measured at W 60 and 300 only (2.1), with T3a's loss-from-step-0 as the nearest measured analog at W 200 (2.3).
